Generated image provenance

All 103 AI images were generated specifically for Real or Render using OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool. The 100 replacement cards use ordinary snapshot prompts. Each generation record contains the actual prompt; original PNGs and source hashes are retained in the project. Camera photographs retain their individual Wikimedia Commons authors and source licenses. All image previews use a common display crop. New generated images were resized and JPEG-recompressed for loading; no generative edits were applied to camera-origin images.
