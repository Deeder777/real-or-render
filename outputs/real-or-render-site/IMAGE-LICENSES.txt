All 353 AI images were generated specifically for Real or Render using OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. The original 103 AI cards are retained, and 250 new ordinary-snapshot images were generated on 22 October 2026. Every generated card has an actual generation prompt and source hash; original PNGs are retained in the project.

353 camera-origin photographs retain their individual authors, source file pages and licenses. Each camera card links to its attribution and license record. The 250 new camera images were conventionally cropped to 4:3, resized to 960×720 and recompressed as JPEG at quality 84, just like the new generated images. The interface crops previews to a shared frame; inspection shows the full image. No generative edits were applied to camera photographs.
